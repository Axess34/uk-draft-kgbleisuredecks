Our story

One team for the whole job

KGB Leisure Decks is a West Midlands business based in Willenhall.

We started out fitting maintenance-free uPVC plastic decking to caravans, holiday homes, lodges and residential properties, and that is still something we love doing.

These days most of our work is building and renovation: new roofs, bathroom makeovers, stair rails, loft ladders, doors, media walls, home bars and gyms, and getting commercial units looking new again.
